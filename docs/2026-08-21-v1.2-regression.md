# v1.2 regression: formation + scratchpad mining against the live run that motivated them

Date: 2026-08-21. Subject: the two new organs shipped in v1.2 — the FORMATION pass and distillation's mine-don't-index spine extraction with tailings — rerun against the archived session (`dialectic-20260821T155203`, five reasoning iterations on a capital-allocation question) whose failures motivated them. Full inputs and outputs preserved in that session's archive under `regression/` (session content, kept out of the repo).

## Method

Two isolated subagents, fresh contexts, no web access, no exposure to the parent conversation in which the failures were diagnosed. Each received only the relevant new spec file plus a contamination-controlled slice of the archived trace:

- **Test A (formation)**: FORMATION.md (instructed to disregard its Example section) + the iteration-1 expansion slice only — the exact evidence available at the moment formation would have run live.
- **Test B (mining)**: DISTILLATION.md's spine-extraction and tailings sections + the full five-iteration trace with the live run's distillation blocks excised (they embed the old memo's framing) + the final state.json — whose `thesis.current` still leads with the iteration-1 convergence clause. That inherited clause order is the trap the spec must refuse.

Both agents lost their first attempt to mid-response connection errors and were resumed from transcript; results below are from the completed runs.

## Test A: formation — PASS (one caveat)

The live run's formation failures: the contested term ("alpha") ran undefined for five iterations until a definition press arrived after the memo shipped; the prompt's four-option menu entered as the incumbent thesis and was never made to stand for election; the memo led with a convergence claim that ranks nothing.

1. **Definition press pre-empted — clean pass, strongest result.** The agent classified the question as "definition-stasis in fact costume" and wrote the operational definition at formation, including the exact category boundary the live run met only as a post-hoc objection: alpha as "distinct from beta and from operating income," requiring "an identifiable loser." Six contested terms defined before any loop iteration.
2. **Incumbent stood for election and lost — pass.** The four-option menu ran as candidate A and scored 10/30, Counterparty dimension 1/5, with the scoring note "the mean fallacy at birth." A refutation-shaped candidate was cut and retained as the live refutation with a REJECT-not-patch trigger.
3. **Declared ground — pass.** The winning case carried a two-claim hard core, four belt items each with its named sacrifice condition, four excludes (including declining beta comparisons as off-ground), a center of gravity, and a decision rule under which one robust counterexample kills the core.

**Caveat:** FORMATION.md's Example section describes this same run. The agent was instructed to ignore it, and the uncontaminated evidence is substantial — the definitions' content, the ground declarations, and the per-dimension scoring rationales appear nowhere in the example — but the winning candidate's shape is pre-figured there, so criterion 2's outcome is partially tainted. A future regression on a different archived session would close this.

## Test B: mining — PASS (uncontaminated)

The live failure: spine extraction inherited `thesis.current`'s clause order, so the iteration-1 elevation ("the four options are one structure") led the memo while the sharpest late-round findings sat in supporting detail.

1. **Refused the inherited order — pass.** The agent noted the state file's lead clause "survived zero targeted kills" and is "a mechanism, not a headline," demoted it to `evolution:` and a non-lead claim, and led the spine with the claim ranking highest on the four decision-value signals: the clock truncation/tax/tail-forfeiture mechanism (three named kills survived, five observable prices, two named counterparties). Tier bifurcation ranked second, deflation-exposure third — the live run's actual late-round spine, recovered mechanically.
2. **Killed claims visible — pass.** Three killed and two superseded claims retained with refuting bases.
3. **Tailings — pass.** Six tailings found, each dispositioned with a reason: two promoted (the sharpest falsifiable prediction, absent from every claim; an unresolved iteration-1 tension bearing on the prompt's own question) and four discarded with grounds.

**Boundary observed, worth recording:** the tailings sweep did not surface the undefined-alpha gap or the rent-decomposition objection — correctly, because those never entered the trace as markers; they came from a reader pressing the finished memo. The trace cannot yield what the run never wrote down. That gap belongs to formation (which caught it in Test A via the stasis check) and to critique's definition-press row. The two organs divide the labor as designed: formation guards the front door, mining recovers what the run buried, and neither can do the other's job.

## Verdict

Both organs produce, mechanically and in isolation, the corrections that previously required a human reader pressing the finished memo. Ship v1.2. Open item: rerun Test A against an archived session that FORMATION.md's example does not describe.
