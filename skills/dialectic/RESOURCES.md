# Resources

What the mechanisms were built from. One line per source: what the plugin took from it, and where that runs. Nothing is listed for pedigree — a text with no mechanism pointer does not appear here.

The longer argument behind the distillation constraints lives in `../../PHILOSOPHICAL-FOUNDATIONS.md`.

## In force

- **Chamberlin, "The Method of Multiple Working Hypotheses," *Science* (1890).** Every frame carries rival hypotheses, one of them the null; a frame that was never rivaled was never tested. `EXPANSION.md`, Phase 1 — unnamed in file.
- **Langlois & Seignobos, *Introduction to the Study of History* (1898).** External source criticism: the position and incentive markers, and the rule that an absence claim needs a targeted fetch that searched for the thing and missed. `MARKERS.md`, `EXPANSION.md` — both run it unnamed.
- **Heuer, *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis* (1999), ch. 8.** Diagnosticity only: evidence counts for what it rules out, so a finding consistent with every live hypothesis moves no confidence. The ACH matrix itself was declined as ritual. `COMPRESSION.md`.
- **Popper, *Conjectures and Refutations* (1963).** The Survival probe and the credit rule under it — R rises only in a round where a kill was attempted and the thesis came through it — plus the warrant for treating REJECT as a finished result. `CRITIQUE.md`, named inline.
- **Lakatos, *Proofs and Refutations* (1976); "Falsification and the Methodology of Scientific Research Programmes" (1970).** The progressive/degenerating test inside the amputation check: a change that only excuses the counter is a thesis dying, not adapting. `CRITIQUE.md` — unnamed in file.
- **Hegel, *Science of Logic* (1812–1816), §185–§187 on *Aufhebung*.** ELEVATE and the Preservation Gate: what a counter proved must be carried into the successor thesis, and amputation is the name for when it wasn't. `CRITIQUE.md` Elevation Test, `DISTILLATION.md` Tension probe.
- **Fisher, "On the Mathematical Foundations of Theoretical Statistics" (1922).** Sufficiency is relative to an inference, which fixes the memo's target: compress for the reader's decision, not for the analysis. `DISTILLATION.md` Sufficiency probe.
- **Tufte, *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information*, 2nd ed. (2001), ch. 6.** Data-ink becomes conviction-ink: a sentence earns its place by advancing the argument, carrying evidence, or naming risk. `DISTILLATION.md`, `SYNTHESIS.md`.
- **Aristotle, *Rhetoric* I.2 (1356a–1356b).** The *propositio* is an enthymeme — the conclusion with just enough argument for the reader to reconstruct the rest. `SYNTHESIS.md`.
- **Cicero, *De Inventione* I.19–I.78; Quintilian, *Institutio Oratoria* IV–VI.** Classical *dispositio* supplies the memo's section order, and the *refutatio* is the section that killed claims must appear in. `SYNTHESIS.md`, `DISTILLATION.md` — the Promotion Formatting table translates the jargon out at the reader boundary.
- **Kent, "Words of Estimative Probability," *Studies in Intelligence* (1964).** Fixed bands, fixed words, deviation forbidden: verdict vocabulary is bounded by the state file. House variance — the band reads off the *lowest* of R, E, and C rather than a single estimative scale, because the corpus forbids a composite. `SYNTHESIS.md` — unnamed in file.
- **Pollock, "Defeasible Reasoning," *Cognitive Science* 11 (1987).** Rebutting versus undercutting: the holdout must say whether a counter denies the claim or breaks the inference to it. `HOLDOUT.md` Pass 2c, consumed downstream by `FORGE.md`.
- **Christensen, *The Innovator's Dilemma* (1997).** The disruption pattern's detection signals and its critique implications, used as a seeder rather than a theory. `PATTERNS.md`.

## Demoted

The rule survives; the name no longer runs in the probe.

- **Kolmogorov, "Three Approaches to the Definition of the Quantity of Information" (1965).** Shortest description that preserves structure — the Compression Gate's sentence-removal question is what remains. Informs the spirit of earned brevity; no longer cited in the probe.
- **Orwell, "Politics and the English Language" (1946).** Cut the word that can be cut; active voice shows agency. Absorbed into Conviction-Ink's failure modes; no longer cited in the probe.
- **Eilenberg & Mac Lane, "General Theory of Natural Equivalences" (1945).** Compress the nodes, not the edges. The Trace probe's bidirectional check states the constraint without the functors; no longer cited in the probe.
- **Miller, "The Magical Number Seven, Plus or Minus Two" (1956).** Chunking sets the Threads probe's cap at three. The cap runs; the 7±2 narration does not.
- **Pitman–Koopman–Darmois theorem** (Darmois 1935; Koopman 1936; Pitman 1936). Some problems admit no finite sufficient statistic, which is the honest warrant for having an Escape Hatch — but the hatch fires on iteration count and confidence, not on exponential families.

## Declined, kept as grounding

Scouted in the 2026-08-19 curation audit and not adopted; each grounds a rule the corpus already holds.

- **Mill, *On Liberty* (1859), ch. 2.** Grounds the refutatio test the corpus already states more sharply — would a smart opponent feel their best argument was represented? `SYNTHESIS.md`.
- **Platt, "Strong Inference," *Science* (1964).** The crucial experiment: CONTINUE must name the specific data that resolves the gap, so a search discriminates between live hypotheses instead of accumulating support. `CRITIQUE.md`, decision table. Declined as a rule of its own — the requirement is already written.
- **Keynes, *A Treatise on Probability* (1921), ch. 6.** E measures weight of evidence, not balance; read that way, `COMPRESSION.md`'s `contradicting evidence → E − 0.15` runs backwards. Declined for v1 because the current semantics work in the forensic record, and flagged as a v2 repair.

One tradition in force has no line here on purpose: the adversarial steelman in `HOLDOUT.md` Pass 2 is modern practice with no canonical text, and attaching a name to it would be the erudition, not the omission.
